How to read this

Class is by role. Owner class: founders, anyone who controls a firm, CEOs, executives, and people living on dividends alone. Working class: staff drawing a wage, with or without shares. Out of work: everyone else. It is recalculated every week.

Unhappy means wellbeing below 0.5. Wellbeing is half security, plus needs met and the condition of the home and appliances they rent.

Lines are quarterly averages, ending on the final week itself. A class line is averaged over the people in that class. Where a class had nobody in it, its line breaks and a strip under the chart marks those quarters. Nothing is missing from the record there. Columns are one per year. Every chart has a table of its numbers behind the Table button.
