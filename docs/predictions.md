# Predictions on record before any run

Written 12 November 2026, before the first run of the model. Kept so that neither of us can tune the model toward what we already believed.

## Failure definition (Jason, 12/11/2026)
The run fails the first week that 90% of all agents are unhappy.

## Jason's prediction (12/11/2026)
What companies are doing now (moving to subscriptions, keeping ownership of what people pay for) is not sustainable. If it continues on its present path it will eventually collapse in on itself. Open source is the opposite and would be more constructive for society, but if it is too open it may collapse as well.

## Claude's prediction (12/11/2026)
Neither world collapses by itself. The outcome of the subscription world turns on who holds the shares. Expect a drift toward monopoly, and toward concentrated ownership through distress selling. Expect the town to look healthy while arrivals are bringing money in, and to show its character after the population reaches 1,000.

## How this is used
Every result in the paper is reported against these two statements, whichever way it falls.
